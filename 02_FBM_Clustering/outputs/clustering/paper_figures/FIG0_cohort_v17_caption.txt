FIG 0   ·   the task, through one electrode per type   (the paper's Figure 1)
====================================================================================================

THE TASK
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Three naming conditions: a spoken definition (audio), a picture, a written sentence to
complete (sentences). Each trial is fixation, stimulus, then a response cue ("?") and the
spoken answer. The time axis is WARPED, not clock time: stimulus and response each occupy
half a block (proportions 0, 0.5, 0.5), the response cue falls at mid-block (dashed), and
the fixation screen contributes no bins - the baseline every dB value is expressed against
comes from it. This is the axis every centroid and every cube in the paper is drawn on;
under the bottom cube it is labelled as FIG 2 labels it, % of trial per condition: 0 and
50 in each block, 100 at the end.

THE HEADER - each condition's trial over its own block
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Over each condition's block, its stimulus screen over the stimulus half and the "?"
response cue over the response half: the speaker with the fixation cross that stays on
screen while the definition is spoken, the picture, the written sentence. The fixation
screen before the stimulus has no bins on this axis and is not drawn. The picture is the
paradigm figure's own drawing (stim_picture.png in paper_figures/assets/); the
sentence is the paradigm figure's example.

THE ROWS - one electrode per type, and one visual electrode
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each type's row is its most representative electrode in the main solution (15 bands,
convex NMF, K = 7): the highest correlation with the type's mean among electrodes with a
loading of at least 0.5 on it, PAT_3455 left out for its broadband burst. Two are chosen
by hand: PAT_5533 IPD3 for the audio-stimulus type, which the clustering itself gives no
loading on that type (beta decrease 0.37, low-frequency increase 0.36, response broadband increase 0.27), and
a visual electrode, EL043 IOG7, which stands for no type and is framed grey. The
response-period decrease, low-frequency increase and sentence-stimulus types have no row.
Each row: the electrode alone on the lateral view of its own hemisphere, in its colour,
and its three conditions concatenated, 0-400 Hz, on the project's +-7 dB scale, framed
in the same colour. Nothing is written beside the rows; top to bottom they are:

THE GATE, as the cache that applied it recorded it
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each electrode-condition ERSP - 103 frequencies x 300 time-normalised bins = 30900 bins,
0-400 Hz, dB re the pre-stimulus baseline - the electrode-condition passes if
  at least 2% of bins are above +2.2 dB (618 bins), OR
  at least 4% of bins are below -3.0 dB (1236 bins).
Since cache schema 3 (2026-09-23) a bin counts as above only beyond max(+2.2 dB, 2 x noise) and as below only beyond min(-3.0 dB, -2 x noise),
noise = sd(half1 - half2) / 2 of that electrode-condition (the trial average's own noise, ~1/sqrt(N)).
The per-row thresholds are the cache's thr_pos_used / thr_neg_used; the example panels draw those.
An electrode is KEPT if any of its three conditions passes. The rule counts bins
over a threshold across the whole cube: it is not a high-gamma measure and it is
not a test of whether the response repeats. Re-derived from the stored proportions,
it reproduces the stored flag on every row; all 1587 cohort electrodes come out kept.
