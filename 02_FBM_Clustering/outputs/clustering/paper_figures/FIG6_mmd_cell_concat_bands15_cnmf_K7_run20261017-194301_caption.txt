WHAT IS MEASURED
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unbiased MMD^2 (Gaussian kernel, bandwidth = median pairwise distance over the cohort,
1.3327) between every pair of clusters, on the unit-norm rows the methods fit in.
In-sample: against 2000 within-patient shuffles of the two labels (BH over the pairs).
Held-out: 50 random halves of the patients; the method refitted on half A, aligned to the
published clusters on half A (Hungarian), half B placed by the LOPO rule, MMD^2 on half B; the
Gaussian cloud (data covariance, no clusters) through the identical procedure, 50 draws.
The number in the matrices is the median held-out MMD^2 divided by the median cloud MMD^2. The cloud's
sectors are convex pieces of one featureless distribution, so they are well apart by construction:
a ratio below 1 means the two types overlap more than two such sectors, above 1 less. The dot marks
the within-patient shuffle on the held-out half: p < 0.05 in at least 90 % of the splits (z_ho is its
median z). The in-sample z and q are circular (the labels were fitted on these electrodes) and are
reported for completeness.
Two versions: argmax members (every count and test of the paper) and members at loading >= 0.3
(the drawn set, 84.5 % of the electrodes). The cloud is cut by the same
FRACTION, not the same threshold: its loadings are flatter and a threshold would remove half of it.
The gap between the versions is the sensitivity of the separation to the cut.
The maps are the standardised mean difference in dB per bin, outlined where the within-patient
shuffle gives q < 0.05 over the bins; the bars are each band's share of the LINEAR-kernel MMD^2
(squared mean difference in the kernel space). The Gaussian-kernel MMD has no per-bin split.
