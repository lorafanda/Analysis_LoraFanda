FIG 4  Cluster correspondence, K = 8.

Clusters from two solutions are PAIRED by the correlation between their mean 5 bands z responses, each electrode z-scored across its own features first and the mean across a solution's clusters removed, and the pairing is TESTED on how many electrodes the paired clusters share. The two quantities are independent: nothing about a shape correlation forces two clusters to hold the same electrodes.

Every cluster is described in the same space (concat_bands5z, from concat_source_v6, 1688 electrodes) whatever feature set produced it, so a clustering fitted on HFA and one fitted on 15 bands can be compared at all. Cells give the Jaccard overlap of the matched pair; stars are the permutation p against the plain null (1000 draws, cluster sizes kept, the matching redone in every draw). * p<0.05, ** p<0.01, *** p<0.001.
