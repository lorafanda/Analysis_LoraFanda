THE GATE, as the cache that applied it recorded it
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
For each electrode-condition ERSP - 129 frequencies x 300 time-normalised bins = 38700 bins,
0-400 Hz, dB re the pre-stimulus baseline - the electrode-condition passes if
  at least 2% of bins are above +2.2 dB (774 bins), OR
  at least 4% of bins are below -3.0 dB (1548 bins).
An electrode is KEPT if any of its three conditions passes. The rule counts bins
over a threshold across the whole cube: it is not a high-gamma measure and it is
not a test of whether the response repeats. Re-derived from the stored proportions,
it reproduces the stored flag on every row; all 1693 cohort electrodes come out kept.

WHAT 'TOTAL' MEANS, AND WHAT HAPPENED TO EVERY ELECTRODE IN THE TABLE
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The total in A is what the gate SAW: the ungated table for the cohort's patients,
minus what lf_concat removes before the gate - non-neural channels, grid and
microelectrode contacts, excluded patients - and minus electrodes missing a
condition, which the gate never sees whole. Those filters are the pipeline's own,
imported, and the total splits exactly into the cohort and the gate's rejects:
